Privacy policy.

How information entered on this website is handled when you contact AK Garments.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Information you provide

The wholesale enquiry form may ask for your name, business or shop name, phone or WhatsApp number, city, interested category, estimated quantity, preferred contact time and message. Please only provide information relevant to your enquiry.

How the enquiry form works

When you submit the form, this website prepares a message containing the details you entered and opens WhatsApp using a click-to-chat link. You can review the message before sending it. The website itself does not claim to store form submissions in a database.

WhatsApp and third-party services

If you choose to send the prepared message, your conversation is handled through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp/Meta's own terms and privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing information through that service.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally add advertising trackers or analytics tools as part of the enquiry form. Your browser or hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver the website.
